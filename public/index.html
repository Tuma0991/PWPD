What it does

Everything you need to keep them safe

GuardianNako turns the small GuardianNemo wearable into a safety net for the people you care about most.

Automatic Fall Detection

A 100 Hz sensor monitors movement patterns and detects a fall the instant it happens — no button press needed. A 15-second “I’m OK” window cancels false alarms.

One-Tap SOS Button

A long press on the device sends an urgent SOS alert to every emergency contact at the same time.

Live GPS Tracking

See their real-time location on a map and follow a road route straight to them.

Safe-Zone Alerts

Draw a boundary on the map and get notified the moment they leave it.

Instant Emergency Alerts

Push notification and SMS to every trusted contact — even when the app is closed.

Monitor Multiple Devices

Keep an eye on every family member from one account.
