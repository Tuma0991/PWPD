Guardian

IoT Wearable Protection Device

Guardian is a wearable that watches over you — and the people you love. It detects falls and emergencies in real time, then instantly alerts your trusted contacts by push notification and SMS with your live location.

Fall Detection

On-device accelerometer automatically detects falls the moment they happen.

SOS Button

One long-press triggers an instant emergency alert to your contacts.

Live GPS Tracking

See the wearer's real-time location on a map.

Geofence Alerts

Get notified the moment the wearer leaves a safe zone.

Emergency Alerts

Push notification + SMS to every emergency contact.

Multi-Device

Pair and monitor multiple devices from a single account.

Download for Android

Tap the button, then open the downloaded file and allow “Install from unknown apps” to install. A Play Store version is coming soon.

How it works

  1. Wear the Guardian device.
  2. Pair it with the app over Bluetooth.
  3. If a fall or SOS is triggered, your contacts are alerted instantly with your location.
